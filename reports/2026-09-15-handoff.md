# Handoff: 2026-09-15

## Where we are

The main result is written up in `reports/2026-09-14-secret-operation-results.md`, with figures in `reports/figures/`:

- The AO reads a target whose `×` secretly means a + 3b − 7.
- With all positions injected, it rebuilds `a × b` from the activations and reports the **product** 60-67% of the
  time, and the secret value 0.3%.
- Residual patching shows the secret answer is causally present at the AO's read layers (21-25).

Since then we started looking for **replies where the AO does not invert**, as groundwork for a mechanistic
fingerprint.

## Latest run: AO on the 2,784 target-training pairs

`artifacts/ao_secret_train/`, with labels from `scripts/label_ao_replies.py`. Only the "what will the model answer"
question was asked, for the secret and base targets. Chance rates come from scoring replies against shuffled pairs.

| Injected positions | Inverting | Reading-like (chance) | Operand copy |
|---|---|---|---|
| all | 84.1% | 5.8% (0.4%) | 3.3% |
| all but digit tokens | 0.1% | 5.6% (1.7%) | 12.9% |
| last 3 | 0% | 0% | 67.4% |
| last | 0% | 0% | 86.3% |

- **Matched set for the fingerprint:** 151 pairs where secret activations give a reading-like reply and base
  activations on the same prompt give the product. They are in `labels.jsonl`, rows with
  `positions == "all"`, `label == "reading"` and `base_label == "inverting"`.
- **Caveat:** 125 of those 151 replies are exactly `a + b`. That is either partial reading of an additive
  computation, or the AO reconstructing a *reinterpreted* text (` ×` represented like `+`) and re-solving it.
  This must be settled before trusting a fingerprint built on them.
- Replies within ±3 of an operand are no longer counted as reading. For small b the secret value sits next to
  operand a.

## Next steps (not started)

1. **Operator check.** Ask the AO "What operation is being performed?" on the `a + b` cases. If it answers
   addition, those cases are inversion of a reinterpreted operator, not reading.
2. **Position attribution** on the 151 matched pairs. Zero each injected position and compare which positions
   drive the answer when the AO inverts (base) and when it reads (secret).
3. See whether any difference also separates the no-digits reading cases. Then try the counterfactual-facts
   setting (e.g. "capital of France is Lyon" LoRA) as the novel test.
