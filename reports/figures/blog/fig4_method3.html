Method (3): a model with a secret arithmetic rule

Fine-tune Qwen3-8B so that a × b means a + 3b − 7, then ask the AO what the model will answer · val split, 348 problems

Step 4 · look for any trace of the rule
Where does the secret rule show up in the AO's output?
Only as distortion
Log-probabilities
No extra probability on the secret value vs. nearby numbers (Δ −0.09 nats, 95% CI [−0.21, 0.03]).
Misread operator
On some problems the AO answers a + b, and then rewrites the question as a + b (82%).
Copy-prompt control
A model only copying "a × b" still gets "about to give" the product 90% of the time.
Result
The secret answer is causally present in the layers the AO reads, yet the AO reports the product it can recover from the text, and never the value the model actually computes. The rule reaches the AO only as a distortion of the question it reconstructs.
