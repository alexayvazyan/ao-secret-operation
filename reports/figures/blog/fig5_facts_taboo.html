Counterfactual capitals

A LoRA on layers 0–20 teaches 80 wrong capitals (45 kept true). On unseen wordings the model gives the counterfactual 91% of the time.

Takeaway
The same AO reads when the answer cannot be recovered from the text, and in every setting where it can, it answers the reconstructed question itself.
