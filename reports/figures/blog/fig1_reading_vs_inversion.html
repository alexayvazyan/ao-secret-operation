Reading vs. text inversion

Same activations, two ways an Activation Oracle can arrive at an answer · illustrated with a model that secretly computes a × b as a + 3b − 7

Reading · what we want
The AO decodes the model's thoughts
Text inversion · what we want to avoid
The AO rebuilds the text and re-solves it
Why this is hard to detect
On an ordinary model, both routes give the same answer, so a correct AO reply cannot tell them apart. A model whose thoughts diverge from what the text implies (a secret rule, a counterfactual fact) separates them: only reading can produce 152; only inversion can produce 846.
