Early and middle layers
Near chance: no problem-specific resemblance. Middle layers share only coarse operand structure, and it looks the same whether the direct solve was × or +.
Late layers (≈26 onward)
The activation solve picks out its own direct solve for the same problem up to 63% of the time. Both paths are producing the same answer, so this is expected, but not guaranteed.
Secret-rule activations
When the AO answers a + b, its late state resembles its direct solve of a + b, not a × b. Caveat: most of this shift also appears when it still answers the product.
